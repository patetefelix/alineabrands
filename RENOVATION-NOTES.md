# Alinea editorial renovation

Replace the site files in the repository root, retaining the existing images folder. CNAME preserves alineabrands.net. All contact links and the inquiry draft use felix@alineabrands.net; the mailbox must be configured with your email provider separately.

Manrope is the only text font. The brand’s supplied SVG wordmark remains unchanged. Footer color is static; the turning hero icon and shelf tilt have been removed.

Lies for Sale and Prisma are in the project collection with typographic cover treatments until their artwork is supplied. No speculative images or business results have been added. Costella and Solferino each have a View live demo button at the top of their project page. Existing Costella filenames and grids are preserved.

This package updates website files only; it does not change DNS or email hosting.
