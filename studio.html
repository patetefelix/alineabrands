Alinea was founded in 2023 as a collaborative effort by four friends, family, and partners — Luicelis, Félix, Diego, and Paola. We met while working at a large design firm, and quickly realized we shared a passion for creating work that was both beautiful and meaningful. We started our own studio so we could have more control over our work and focus on brands we were truly passionate about. Since founding, Alinea has worked with a wide range of clients, from small startups to larger companies.
