Before we start

A few good
questions.

Who do you work with?

Founders and teams creating or refreshing food, beverage, and everyday consumer brands. Share your idea and we’ll explore whether the project is a good fit.

What can a project include?

Brand strategy, visual identity, packaging, illustration, and art direction. The scope follows what your brand needs.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, number of products, and timeline. We’ll prepare a proposal around your brief.

Are the concept brands real businesses?

Self-initiated projects are fictional brands created to explore and demonstrate our approach. They are clearly labeled; they don’t imply real production, distribution, or sales.
